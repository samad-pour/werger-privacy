Wergêr Support

Help for the Wergêr app on iPhone, iPad and Android

Wergêr converts Kurdish text between the Latin and the Arabic script. If something does not work as expected, or you have a question or an idea, write to us. We usually answer within a few days.

  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

Frequently asked questions

Does Wergêr work without an internet connection?

Yes. Converting text always works offline. Speech recognition and read-aloud also work offline once the speech model and voices are on your device. Handwriting recognition needs a connection once per script to download its model.

The offline speech model or voices do not arrive

The App Store or Google Play delivers these files, the same way it delivers app updates. The voices come by themselves after installing; the speech model is downloaded from the Wergêr Pro screen. Make sure you are connected to the internet (Wi-Fi for large downloads), have enough free storage, and installed the app from the App Store or Google Play. Then open the app again and wait a moment. On iPhone and iPad, offline speech needs iOS 26 or later.

Where are my history and saved conversions?

They are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are kept when the app is updated, but deleting the app deletes them, and they do not move to a new device.

How do I restore Wergêr Pro on a new device or after reinstalling?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, open the Wergêr Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wergêr. Or see Apple's guide.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Wergêr. Or see Google's guide.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund page.

A word is converted wrongly

Please send us the word or sentence and the result you expected. Every report helps improve the conversion for everyone.
