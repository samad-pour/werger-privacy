Delete your Wergêr data

Wergêr by besam, for iPhone, iPad and Android

Wergêr has no accounts. Almost all your data stays on your device, and our servers store nothing you type, say or photograph. This page explains how to delete what there is.

How to request deletion

  1. Write an e-mail to samad.pour.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Wergêr: delete my data”.
  2. If you bought Wergêr Pro, include the order number of your purchase. You find it in the receipt e-mail from Google Play (it starts with “GPA.”) or from Apple. Without it we cannot find your purchase record, because Wergêr does not know your name or e-mail address.
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Data on your device

Your history, your saved conversions, and the speech model and voices are stored only on your device. You can delete history and saved conversions in the app at any time. Uninstalling Wergêr deletes all of it. We cannot access this data.

What is deleted on request

  • Subscription record: the Wergêr Pro purchase record kept by our subscription service RevenueCat: the store's purchase receipt, a random identifier the app created, and basic device and app information.

After deletion, the app may create a new random identifier the next time it checks your subscription. An active subscription keeps working: tap “Restore purchases” on the Wergêr Pro screen.

What is not stored, and what is kept

  • Voice recordings, photos and texts sent for speech recognition, photo reading or read-aloud are processed for that one request and discarded at once. Nothing is stored, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases and payments under their own policies. We cannot delete those; see your Apple ID or Google Play account.
